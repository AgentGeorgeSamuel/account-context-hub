# Scope, evidence and next steps

## Included in the project design

- Account-relevant email and privacy-gated Teams content ingestion.
- Conservative contact/account resolution.
- Source-grounded extraction of summaries, rationale and actions.
- Dataverse conversation context and timeline notes.
- Source-based replay handling and missing-note reconciliation.
- Scheduled/manual account-brief generation with bounded evidence.
- Decision DNA query modes: Account360, PlayFinder and PortfolioScan.
- Explicit source references, ambiguity handling and opportunity-state distinctions.

## Included in this public showcase

- Interactive conceptual architecture.
- Four prose flow definitions and step walkthroughs.
- Fictional example responses that demonstrate intended behavior.
- Project value, boundaries and suggested measurement criteria.
- Static presentation assets only; no connected Microsoft services.

## Historical project validation

Saved project records describe successful controlled email and posted-Teams-summary ingestion, correct CRM linking, duplicate-free replay, demo account briefs, query-mode tests and Copilot Studio publication. A targeted evaluation checked that positive interest was not misrepresented as a won opportunity.

This is a summary of prior validation, not a fresh tenant audit or an exhaustive evaluation. Private records and test links are intentionally absent from the public repository.

## Not complete or not established

- **Newly recorded meeting transcript ingestion:** not live-verified. Posted-summary success is not transcript-path success.
- **Long-source processing:** oversized Teams text is rejected; tested chunking is future work.
- **Unbounded recovery:** polling/lookback is bounded; longer outages require explicit backfill.
- **Instantaneous briefs:** new interactions and rebuilt account summaries have different refresh timing.
- **Production readiness:** no claim of load, scale, reliability, security certification, legal compliance or tenant-wide access-control assessment.
- **Measured ROI:** no demonstrated percentage improvement, revenue uplift or time-saving metric is published.

## Out of scope for publication

Tenant/environment identifiers, URLs, identity details, customer content, original screenshots, connector bindings, flow exports, executable expressions, prompt source, agent configuration, solution packages and private verification logs.

## Next validation priorities

1. Exercise a newly recorded meeting and verify actual transcript provenance end to end.
2. Design and test long-source chunking with completeness and provenance checks.
3. Test failure recovery and explicit backfill across longer outages.
4. Expand query evaluation across ambiguity, missing evidence, contradictory context and deal states.
5. Review permissions, retention, DLP, monitoring, licensing and capacity before production use.
6. Establish baseline business measures and assess improvements with representative users.
