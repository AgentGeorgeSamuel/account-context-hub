# Business value

## From fragmented history to usable account memory

An interaction summary is only useful when it reaches the right account, retains its source and can be found at the moment of a decision. Decision DNA combines those responsibilities rather than treating summarization as the whole solution.

### Account preparation

**Before:** manually reconstruct the technical discussion, objections and next steps across channels.

**With the hub:** begin from source-linked interactions and a current brief, then inspect original evidence where needed.

### Handoffs and continuity

**Before:** decision context depends on the current account owner's memory.

**With the hub:** a new owner can review what happened, why it mattered, the opportunity's actual state and outstanding actions.

### Reusable decision evidence

**Before:** a won opportunity contains an outcome but limited discoverable context.

**With the hub:** recorded decision reasons and interactions can support play discovery. Correlation is not proof that a particular action caused a win.

### Portfolio attention

**Before:** risks and promises remain buried in individual communications.

**With the hub:** query modes bring supported signals and actions into review, with bounded evidence and human judgment retained.

### Trust and governance

Conservative linking, replay handling and source-bearing notes reduce the risk of wrong-account summaries and repeated records. These controls support trust; they do not constitute a compliance certification or guarantee that AI output is correct.

## How to measure success

No performance, adoption or financial improvement is claimed by this showcase. A deployment could measure:

| Measure | Suggested evaluation |
|---|---|
| Preparation effort | Compare time spent on the same preparation task before/after, with answer completeness held constant |
| Source traceability | Review whether material claims link to supporting records and whether cited evidence actually supports them |
| Correct association | Audit account/contact mapping against an independently reviewed sample |
| Duplicate prevention | Replay known sources and compare persisted insight/note counts |
| Handoff completeness | Ask incoming owners to recover decisions, rationale and open actions from the account record |
| Coverage and freshness | Compare source arrival, ingestion and latest successful brief rebuild timestamps |
| Query reliability | Evaluate ambiguous accounts, missing evidence, failed lookups and open/won/lost distinctions |

Benefits depend on source quality, permissions, adoption, operational reliability and review practices.
